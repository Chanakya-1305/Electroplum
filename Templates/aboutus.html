About Electroplum

Built by tradespeople,run like a technology company

We started as three electricians tired of no-shows and vague quotes. Today we're a licensed network of technicians across Hyderabad, still holding the same standard.

OUR STORY

We got tired of the four-hour arrival window too

Electroplum began in 2019 when our founder, an electrician by trade, kept hearing the same complaint from customers: technicians who didn't show up, quoted one price and charged another, or disappeared halfway through a job.

So we built the company we wished existed — technicians who are actually licensedand background-checked, pricing that's confirmed before any work starts, and a booking system that tells you when someone's coming instead of a vague "sometime today."

What started with three electricians and a shared spreadsheet is now a network of vetted plumbing, electrical and HVAC technicians serving homes and businesses across the city.
